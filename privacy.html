Level Up Fitness

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026
Short version

Level Up Fitness stores workout information on your device. If you choose to create an account, the app also backs up your training data to our Supabase-powered cloud database so it can be restored and synced across devices.

Information Level Up Fitness may process

Depending on the features you use, Level Up Fitness may process your email address, display name, optional profile and training preferences, saved workout plans and schedule, completed workout history, sets, weights, repetitions, workout grades, streak information, exercise notes, app settings, and active-workout recovery data.

Device-only use

You can use the current app without an account. In that case, workout and settings data is stored in your browser on that device. Clearing browser/site storage may remove device-only data.

Accounts and cloud backup

If you create or sign in to an account, Level Up Fitness uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage. Synced data is associated with your account so that it can be restored on another signed-in device.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the workout experience, save your plans and history, calculate progress and training statistics, restore active workouts, show previous exercise notes and performance, keep your settings, sync signed-in accounts, and diagnose reliability problems.

Diagnostics

Level Up Fitness keeps a small local error log to help diagnose app failures. When you are signed in, technical error information may also be sent to our Supabase database. Diagnostic records can include the app build, current screen, error message and stack, browser type, connection status, and viewport size. They are intended for reliability and debugging rather than advertising.

Third-party services

Level Up Fitness currently relies on Supabase for signed-in authentication and cloud storage. The web beta is delivered through GitHub Pages. Exercise demonstrations or other media may be loaded from approved external media sources used by the app.

Sharing and selling

Level Up Fitness does not sell your workout data. Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the app, such as authentication, hosting, cloud storage, and approved media delivery providers.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can request account deletion directly from the Profile screen. Deleting an account removes the account and its associated cloud workout data. The app also removes the signed-in account's local training data from that device as part of the deletion flow.

Data retention

Device-only data remains until you remove it, clear site data, or use an account action that clears the signed-in user's local data. Cloud account data is retained while the account is active and is removed through the account-deletion flow, subject to normal infrastructure backup and security processes.

Children and age requirements

Level Up Fitness is a fitness logging product and is still preparing for public release. Final store age-rating and any age-specific account requirements will be set before public launch.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Level Up Fitness's production services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Support

For current beta help and troubleshooting, visit the Level Up Fitness Support page.
